Balde vs Bagyo
Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026 · Android application: com.baldebagyo.game

Balde vs Bagyo is developed by Albert Castañeto, publishing as koidafish. This policy explains how the game handles information, including when advertising is enabled. For privacy or support questions, email albertcastaneto@gmail.com.

Game information stored on your device

The game stores progress, high scores, character unlocks, and control and audio preferences locally on your device. It does not require an account, and we do not operate a server that receives these game saves. The game does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, or precise device location.

Advertising and third-party services

The Android game integrates Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) and Google’s User Messaging Platform for advertising and privacy choices. Development builds use test advertisements. Ad availability can vary by build, connection, region, and privacy settings.

Google’s advertising services may process network information such as your IP address (which can indicate an approximate location), ad and app interactions, technical diagnostics, and device or app identifiers where permitted by the applicable settings. These services use information to deliver and measure ads, maintain performance, and detect fraud or abuse. Non-personalized ads can still involve data processing; they do not mean that no information is collected.

AdMob data is handled by Google and applicable advertising service providers. Read Google’s Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services for more information.

Children and families

The game is intended for a mixed audience, including children. We do not ask players to submit a name, birth date, email address, or other personal details inside the game. The current advertising configuration applies child-directed treatment and a general-audience ad content rating to all players, disables ad personalization, and requests under-age-of-consent treatment. We do not enable interest-based advertising in this configuration.

These safeguards restrict advertising data use, but advertising services may still process limited technical information for ad delivery, security, and measurement. A parent or guardian who has a question about a child’s information can contact us at the email above.

Privacy choices

Google’s privacy messaging service determines whether a privacy message applies based on the app configuration and relevant region and age-treatment settings. Messages may be suppressed for users treated as under the age of consent. We do not rely on a child giving consent to personalized advertising.

You can manage available advertising privacy controls in your Android or Google account settings. Rewarded ads are optional: choosing to end a round instead of watching an ad does not require you to watch one.

Support messages

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever information you include. We use it to respond and resolve the issue. Please avoid sending sensitive information or a child’s personal details. Support correspondence is retained only as needed to handle the request and meet applicable obligations.

Retention, deletion, and security

You can remove local progress and preferences by clearing the game’s app storage through Android settings. Uninstalling normally removes local app data, although device backup and restore settings may affect retained copies. We cannot retrieve a local save from a developer server.

Google controls retention of information processed by its services under its own policies. You can use Google’s privacy controls or contact Google about that information. Email us to request deletion of support correspondence or ask about access, correction, deletion, or other privacy rights that apply to you. We may need enough information to verify and handle the request.

We take reasonable steps to protect information we handle. Google states that its Mobile Ads SDK encrypts transmitted user data using TLS. No storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security.

Changes and contact

We will update this page when the game’s information practices change and revise the date above. For questions or requests, contact Albert Castañeto (koidafish) at albertcastaneto@gmail.com.
